AUC summarizes the whole ROC curve, but in practice we eventually have to pick one cutoff to actually classify someone. How do you choose that threshold, especially in something like cancer diagnosis where a false negative is much worse than a false positive? And if two models have almost the same AUC, could their ROC curves still cross so that one is better at the cutoff we actually care about?
